# Gothic Candlelight Design System Steering

## Palette & Atmosphere
- **Obsidian Slate Surfaces**: `#0e0f12` (void), `#15171c` (crypt stone), `#1c1f26` (elevated slate).
- **Candlelight Amber Accents**: `#e59a38` (primary flame), `#f2ba66` (warm highlight) for active rituals and primary CTAs.
- **Bone Parchment Typography**: `#efe6d5` (primary text), `#a69e8e` (engraved stone secondary text), `#6e685c` (muted inscriptions).
- **Spectral Sage & Wax Seal**: `#6fb38a` for resurrected/completed rituals; `#c94a4a` for urgent curses or destructive resets.
- **Avoid**: Generic neon purple-on-dark gradients, harsh RGB borders, or cluttered grid overlays. Favor subtle stone noise texture, crisp 1px iron-etched hairlines, and purposeful typography (`Cinzel` display, `Inter` body, `JetBrains Mono` metrics).
